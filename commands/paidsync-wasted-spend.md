# Find wasted spend

1. Call `paidsync_context` and confirm the account and date range (default: last 30 days).
2. On Google Ads, follow `wasted-spend-hunter` and `negative-keyword-triage`. On Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and the other platforms, follow `paidsync-optimize` to find ad sets and ads spending without results.
3. Show the total spend each proposed change would stop.
4. Apply only what the user approves, then read back the result.
